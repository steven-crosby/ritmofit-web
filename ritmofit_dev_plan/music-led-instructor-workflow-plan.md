## Goal and evidence

An instructor can connect a music service, select and arrange music, and teach from a readable Live surface with honest playback readiness. Music drives creation and instruction until the instructor explicitly chooses otherwise ([D24](./decisions.md#d24--music-drives-creation-and-instruction-until-the-instructor-chooses-otherwise-resolved-2026-09-30)).

Evidence: [September 29 benchmark](../docs/audits/instructor-benchmark-2026-09-29/evidence/README.md), [class-score blueprint](./instructor-class-score-blueprint.md), and owner-supplied September 30 iPhone Safari recordings at 10:58 and 11:09. Recordings remain outside git. The owner treats inactivity longer than 10 seconds as suspected friction and confirmed silence during the later Apple Music preparation wait. This is not a universal timeout or proof that every pause is a bug.

| Observation | Evidence boundary | Required outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Music reconnect waits without completion | Owner report and first recording; cause unknown | Bounded, cancellable connection flow with retry and a visible result |
| Provider card taps do not reconnect | Code selects catalog only; capability labels are informational | Explicit provider-specific connect/reconnect action on each card |
| Connected/Authorized precedes failed playback | Second recording: catalog search/add succeeds, later playback fails | Distinct stored connection, library retrieval, browser authorization, and runtime states |
| Silent Apple preparation while class time advances | Second recording around 2:45–3:00; silence confirmed by owner | Teaching position freezes until verified playback or explicit override |
| Text selection across track cards | Second recording around 1:15–1:40 | Predictable tap selection and deliberate reorder controls |
| Phone overflow and hidden teaching information | Both recordings and earlier benchmark frames | Reflowing forms/dialogs and persistent current/next/countdown |
| Class opening/readiness friction | Second recording; user intent during pauses not established | Short path to editing, clear optional preparation versus playback blockers |

Source inspection: `LiveMode` sets its virtual clock running before coordinator start completes; coordinator status is mirrored mainly for display. Adapter `getLiveness` reads official transport position/state but is currently observational. MusicKit connection authorization loads/configures before consent; runtime preparation may authorize again. Async gesture loss, SDK/session state, and blocked consent are investigation hypotheses, not established causes.

Design direction: instructor vocabulary is song, cue, move, effort, chapter, rehearsal, and run of show. Preserve existing ink surfaces, bone teaching text, copper primary action, cyan focus/status, and restrained ember/plasma effort accents. The signature is the music-aligned teaching score and its current/next countdown. Use that hierarchy in place of generic metric-card priority, a dense editor as the entry screen, and motion as a substitute for playback truth. Keep existing token typography and accessible targets.

## Phase 1 — Reliable connections and music-led Live

Deliver narrow slices in the order below. Phase 1 is frontend/runtime work using existing API contracts; no migration, new endpoint, or run-payload change is proposed. If diagnosis establishes a backend/token defect, stop with a separate scoped proposal before changing auth/provider APIs.

### 1A — Diagnose and repair iPhone authorization and connection recovery

- Reproduce on Steven's actual iPhone Safari. Record device/iOS version, Safari versus installed PWA, exact application revision/Worker, provider, intended action, and stage of failure. Observe configuration load, SDK load/configure, consent launch/result, token submission, connection refresh, and return navigation separately. Never capture tokens, cookies, headers, or authentication codes in logs or artifacts.
- Recheck current official MusicKit JS authorization/playback documentation and the applicable provider docs before implementation. Inspect singleton reuse, concurrent configuration/authorization, user-gesture continuity, late completions, background/foreground behavior, and cancelled consent. Diagnose with stage-specific errors; do not prescribe a token reset or Safari-setting workaround as the fix without evidence.
- Share one connection operation between service cards and the existing connections dialog. A card header continues to select its catalog; add a separate labeled Connect/Reconnect action. A connected card offers Manage rather than unsolicited reauthorization. Preserve catalog access when library or playback access is unavailable.
- Prepare SDK/config prerequisites before enabling the Apple authorization action, then invoke consent directly from the explicit tap where the official API permits. Serialize consent/transport ownership; reject superseded results so cancellation, unmount, or provider switching cannot revive old work.
- Show the provider and operation stage immediately. After 10 seconds show slow-operation guidance and Cancel/Retry access. Non-consent SDK/config/network work has a proposed 20-second deadline; human consent retains the existing bounded authorization deadline. Cancel invalidates the operation and prevents token persistence/late playback, even if the provider sheet itself cannot be programmatically closed.
- Carry a validated internal return destination/provider through Spotify/SoundCloud redirects; cancellation and completion return to Music or the originating Builder/Live recovery surface. No arbitrary redirect URLs. On return, refresh connection truth and show success or failure once; do not claim success from navigation alone.
- Show independent library request failures with Retry. Do not label likes/playlists ready solely because an account is connected. Unknown/refreshing capability data stays unknown. This addresses the observed mismatch without inventing SPC-09 backend signals.

**Acceptance:** connect, reconnect, cancellation, timeout, retry, redirect return, and repeated taps work on real iPhone Safari. Apple catalog access and playback authorization are tested separately. No right-edge overflow at 320/375/390px or with Safari keyboard/browser chrome. Provider flow tests cover stale completions, singleton reuse, and usable controls after failure.

### 1B — Clock authority, blocked states, and visible recovery

- Keep one class-position store. In music mode, derive position inside an active song from official provider transport: `track.startOffsetMs + providerPositionMs - track.clipStartMs`, clamped to that playback window. Provider state and progress, not a resolved `play()` promise or host animation loop, establish playing. The host loop may render but cannot independently accumulate teaching time within music.
- Add an internal normalized transport subscription to the adapter contract with track/session identity, provider position, and states playing, paused, buffering, ended, error, or unknown. Implement through official SDK/widget events plus position reads (proposed 250ms foreground polling where events do not suffice, one read in flight). Keep diagnostics/liveness observation separate from operational transport authority; host throttling alone never proves provider failure.
- Preparing, awaiting authorization, buffering, unexpected pause, failed/unknown transport, and transition waits freeze the last confirmed teaching position. Known pause/error/buffering applies immediately; absence of fresh progress freezes extrapolation. After 10 seconds without confirmed progress outside a deliberate pause/gap, surface recoverable stalled/unverified playback with Retry, Manage connection, Skip track, and Continue without music.
- Retry re-enters at the held playback position and resumes only after confirmed transport progress. Instructor Pause during recovery prevents automatic resume. Skip is an explicit jump to the next song's start; its clock remains held until that song plays. No silent skip, provider substitution, or switch to prompter-only after runtime failure.
- Continue without music tears down provider work, invalidates late callbacks, and explicitly resumes the virtual teaching clock at the held position. Run without music at preflight starts that mode directly. This mode persists for the current run; re-entering music requires a deliberate action and new authorization/preparation. Exit/reset destroys outstanding jobs and restores clean initial state.
- A declared free-timeline gap is intentional silence: stop provider playback at the clip boundary and run the scheduled gap countdown on the virtual clock, subject to instructor pause. Hold at the next song's boundary until it plays. Early provider finish before the saved window end is a duration/playback mismatch requiring a decision, not unannounced silence or automatic time advance.
- On background/foreground return, reconcile from current provider position without adding host wall-clock time. Seek/reorder-era stale callbacks cannot move the current job. Preserve throttled React rendering and raw timeline store; establish clock authority tests before changing UI transport labels.
- Put the blocking reason and recovery controls beside transport in the phone viewport. Waiting/error must not display Now teaching or an active Pause state implying verified music. Keep screen wake-lock lifecycle tied to the active teaching/recovery session so a recoverable pause does not silently surrender the teaching screen.

**Acceptance:** silent preparation reproduces with zero teaching/cue/countdown advancement; mid-song stalls hold at the last confirmed position; recovery does not jump ahead. Test provider transitions, clips, seek/reset, manual pause during pending work, early finish, deliberate gaps, unknown telemetry, background return, and stale completions. Prompter-only still progresses without music. SDK transport is not proof of audible Bluetooth output; real-device listening is required.

### 1C — Honest preflight and real-device release gate

Adversarial-review corrections: Spotify paused-at-zero cannot complete a class without observed progress near the source endpoint (within 1 second); ambiguous resets stay held for recovery. Apple/Spotify start commands claim ownership before dispatch, block replacement commands until the original settles, stop/pause immediately on cancellation, and attempt stop/pause again on late completion. SDK calls cannot be forcibly cancelled and stop/pause remains best-effort; an indefinitely pending command requires reload instead of overlapping a retry. Apple configuration retains its underlying serialization after a caller’s 20-second UI deadline, shared by connection and playback paths. Regression tests cover these boundaries. Real-device acceptance remains pending.

Local verification: full CI-equivalent gate passed (997 web tests, 466 API unit tests, 30 music tests, and 171 API integration tests), including build, OpenAPI drift, contract parity, and audit. Synthetic Chrome checks at 320/375/390px passed mixed-provider Apple consent and Spotify activation gating, no hidden audio probe, Spotify activation recovery visibility, provider-held teaching, explicit music-off continuation, and no horizontal overflow. The four original adversarial reproductions also pass after the fixes; equivalent regressions are retained in the repository. These checks do not prove real iPhone audio or interruptions.

- Replace the unconditional hands-free guarantee with separate static track/reference checks and current-browser playback authorization. Label unchecked runtime availability explicitly. Check consent for each required provider before teaching; provider consent belongs behind a visible user gesture rather than an unattended song transition.
- Authorizing/checking must not play class songs as a hidden probe. Start class prepares the first song and holds the teaching position until confirmed playback. Later songs retain runtime failure handling; preflight cannot guarantee future availability. Keep current deterministic initial provider selection and explicit prompter-only choice.
- Run a full Apple Music class on the actual iPhone with Bluetooth output, lock/unlock, and a notification. Also run a mixed SoundCloud → Apple Music class and test retry/skip/override. Record elapsed positions, visible state, audible outcome, and recovery for each scenario.
- If reliable web playback cannot be achieved under the required conditions, retain safe web recovery and report the exact limitation. Native iOS playback requires its own approved plan; do not expand this web slice automatically. Do not present unsupported conditions as ready.

**Gate:** Phase 1 is complete only when code checks pass and the real-iPhone scenarios have retained results. A failed device scenario blocks the corresponding playback claim/release acceptance. Code landing, deployment, and instructor acceptance remain distinct evidence.

## Phase 2 — Phone teaching and editing clarity

- Live prioritizes current cue/move, current countdown, next cue and countdown, with compact track/effort context. Transport and recovery remain reachable. Secondary timeline/ribbon/details use progressive disclosure; landscape must show teaching text rather than controls alone.
- Reserve space for current/next/countdown at default text size. Long cue guidance gets a bounded summary plus an accessible full-text view; do not silently discard authored text. At 200% zoom allow reflow/scrolling instead of shrinking type below accessibility limits. A later per-cue note separates short teaching labels from coaching detail.
- Fix form/dialog/card overflow with flexible widths and wrapping; preserve normal text selection in inputs/notes. Track tap selects and reveals the inspector; reorder uses a deliberate handle and keyboard/numeric alternatives. Limit selection suppression to drag handles/interactive track surfaces where justified by reproduced behavior.
- Make Start a class the clear primary creation action; keep Start empty available. Separate Can run with music / Run without music from optional missing tempo or choreography improvements. Opening a class should reach its run of show without scrolling through redundant metadata; retain title/readiness details as compact, expandable context.
- Validate at 320/375/390px portrait, phone landscape, iPad portrait/landscape, and desktop; include long titles/cues, browser chrome/keyboard, 200% zoom, keyboard use, low light, and reduced motion. Test visible hit targets, focus, and no horizontal page overflow. Owner/instructor validates teaching-distance glanceability.

**Contract impact:** responsive and interaction work uses existing contracts. Discipline-specific effort labels reuse stored enums and require an approved label mapping; do not interpret Struct Club Open as None without instructor clarification.

## Phase 3 — Builder speed, cue notes, and rehearsal

- Cue at playhead reuses existing millisecond anchors and clip math, with keyboard access and a numeric equivalent. Routine copy presents a preview of source cues/moves, destination anchors, and out-of-window conflicts; no silent crop/drop or destructive replacement. Settle copy/undo behavior in a focused interaction proposal before implementation.
- Per-cue coaching notes need an additive nullable field through shared schemas, migration, authorized CRUD, copy paths, run payload, OpenAPI, and future iOS consumer review. Legacy cue text remains intact; no automatic split. Approve note limits and display behavior with the interaction proposal before implementing the contract.
- Rehearsal uses the same teaching projection and clock/recovery behavior as Live: scrub a moment to inspect current/next information, optionally play the selected window through official playback, and return to editing. Rehearsal does not persist simulated time or alter authored data.
- Beats remaining is conditional on instructor validation and known manual tempo/downbeat; seconds remain available. Prototype fractional downbeat entry and seek/pause behavior before choosing rounding/display rules. No Spotify BPM or provider audio analysis.

**Gate:** review each interaction/contract proposal independently. Exercise steps are not needed to ship a truthful preview of existing cues/moves. Test clip/beat anchor conversions, duplicates/copy conflicts, authorization, legacy records, rehearsal/Live equivalence, and absence of unintended writes.

## Phase 4 — Timed steps and the class score

Use the blueprint's 20-minute HIIT stress test with at least two songs, chapters, Hard/Easy steps, precise cues, a timing mismatch, and a gap/clip adjustment. Default to the simple run of show; Show timeline reveals precision lanes and selection inspection.

Before production code, validate step anchoring, cross-song spans, chapter/song movement, clipping, overlap/conflicts, copy, undo, and readiness with novice and experienced instructors. Then propose shared entities, authorized editing APIs, migration, additive run projection, OpenAPI registration, and iOS compatibility. Point cues/moves are not automatically converted into bounded steps. Keep plan target and assembled music duration independent; 20 minutes does not change the recipe catalog.

**Gate:** instructor-tested prototype and a separate approved contract plan. Preserve community deferrals and provider-owned audio boundaries.
